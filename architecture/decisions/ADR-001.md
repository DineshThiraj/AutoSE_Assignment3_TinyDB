# ADR-001: Splitting the work

## Status

Accepted — student choice supplied 2026-11-17. Initial reviewed decision; implementation and experimental evidence pending.

## Context

The assignment fixes six stages and requires A1/A2 reuse plus independent review. Context prose and Nygard formatting are Codex assistance. The choice and quoted explanation below are transcribed from preparation/STUDENT_REVIEW.md (copied here as ../STUDENT_REVIEW.md).

## Decision

Four reasoning roles for requirements, design, implementation/testing and independent review, with deterministic testing and release control.

Student-supplied explanation, labelled “suggested reason” in the source:

> This separates responsibilities without the overhead of six separate reasoning agents, and allows the A1 and A2 artifacts to be reused

## Consequences

Accepted drawback, supplied by the student:

> More hand-offs and model-context overhead.

Considered and rejected alternative:

> One agent handling everything

Student-supplied reason for rejecting it:

> It mixes implementation, verification and review context, making independent review less credible
