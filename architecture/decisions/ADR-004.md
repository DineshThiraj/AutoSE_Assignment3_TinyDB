# ADR-004: State

## Status

Accepted — student choice supplied 2026-11-17. Initial reviewed decision; implementation and experimental evidence pending.

## Context

Actions jobs are disposable; repeated triggers and interrupted publication can produce duplicate work. Context prose and Nygard formatting are Codex assistance. The choice and quoted explanation below are transcribed from preparation/STUDENT_REVIEW.md (copied here as ../STUDENT_REVIEW.md).

## Decision

Immutable execution artifacts, unique attempt identifiers, hashes and a durable run-to-PR index.

Student-supplied explanation, labelled “suggested reason” in the source:

> Failures and interrupted runs should remain traceable, while restarts must check existing branches and PRs to prevent duplicates

## Consequences

Accepted drawback, supplied by the student:

> Additional storage and reconciliation logic.

Considered and rejected alternative:

> Using Actions cache as the authoritative state

Student-supplied reason for rejecting it:

> Cache availability and retention are unsuitable for recovery and grading evidence
