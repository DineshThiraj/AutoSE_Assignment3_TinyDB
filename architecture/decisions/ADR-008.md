# ADR-008: Reviewer independence

## Status

Accepted — student choice supplied 2026-11-17. Initial reviewed decision; implementation and experimental evidence pending.

## Context

Review independence needs restricted inputs and authority, beyond naming a second role. Context prose and Nygard formatting are Codex assistance. The choice and quoted explanation below are transcribed from preparation/STUDENT_REVIEW.md (copied here as ../STUDENT_REVIEW.md).

## Decision

Fresh independent reviewer context, separate prompt, sanitized latest PR source and diff, with no push tools.

Student-supplied explanation, labelled “suggested reason” in the source:

> The reviewer must evaluate the actual change without relying on implementation reasoning, and seeded cases can measure its mistakes

## Consequences

Accepted drawback, supplied by the student:

> Additional cost, latency and possible false positives or missed defects.

Considered and rejected alternative:

> The implementer reviewing its own work in the same conversation

Student-supplied reason for rejecting it:

> Its previous assumptions can influence the outcome
